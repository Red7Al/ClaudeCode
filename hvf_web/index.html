What the Squeeze Scanner does

A purpose-built tool for finding high-probability breakout setups across ~600 instruments (FTSE 100/250, NASDAQ 100, S&P 500, world indices, FX, commodities, crypto).

The idea — a "squeeze"

A squeeze (a volatility funnel) is when price coils into an ever-tighter range: each swing high is a little lower (the red upper jaw H1→H2→H3) and each swing low is a little higher (the green lower jaw L1→L2→L3). The market is compressing — like a spring. When price finally breaks out of the funnel it tends to move fast and far. The scanner finds these funnels and tells you the exact entry, stop and target.

A real setup. The chart marks the three swing highs H1·H2·H3 (red upper jaw) and three swing lows L1·L2·L3 (green lower jaw) converging into the funnel, with the Entry / Stop / Target levels labelled on the right.

The funnel's mouth (H1−L1) is AMP1; the scanner projects the target one AMP1 beyond the midpoint, and the stop just past the opposite jaw — that's how the R:R is computed.

How to use it

• Scanner tab — the full table. Each row is one instrument's best setup. Use the Filters (top-left) or the Search box to narrow it; click a column header to sort.
• Click any row to open the detail panel: the live price chart, the X post card, the rule-by-rule justification, fundamentals, broker trend and the published X thread.
• Pre-orders tab — every setup that hasn't triggered yet (your pending watchlist) with entry / stop / target laid out.
• Colours: green = good / in favour, amber = watch, red = poor / against.
• Refresh data re-scans the whole universe (a few minutes); the page reloads itself when done.

Not financial advice — a research tool. See the Appendix tab for every term explained.

Appendix — glossary of terms

The pattern

Squeeze / HVF funnel — Higher-Velocity Funnel: a volatility contraction where price coils between converging highs and lows before a momentum breakout.
Upper jaw / Lower jaw — the line through the swing highs (descending) and the line through the swing lows (ascending). They converge to form the funnel.
H1, H2, H3 — the three successive swing highs (each lower than the last) that define the upper jaw. H3 is the most recent.
L1, L2, L3 — the three successive swing lows (each higher than the last) that define the lower jaw. L3 is the most recent.
AMP1 — "first amplitude" = H1 − L1, the height of the funnel's mouth. The target is projected one AMP1 beyond the midpoint.

Levels & the trade

Entry — the break level that triggers the setup: H3 for a long, L3 for a short.
Stop — protective exit just beyond the opposite jaw.
Target — projected objective: funnel midpoint ± AMP1.
R:R (Reward : Risk) — (target − entry) ÷ (entry − stop). We look for ≥ 3 : 1.
Dist→Entry — how far (%) the current price is from the entry level.
Since trig — price change since it triggered, measured from the entry level (green = in the trade's favour).

Status & timing

TRIGGERED — price has crossed the entry; actionable now.
READY — funnel complete, waiting for price to cross the entry (a "pre-order").
DEVELOPING — the funnel is still forming.
Triggered (date) — the date the trigger pivot (L3 long / H3 short) formed.
Days since — days since that trigger date.
Timeframe (D240 / D180 / D90 / weekly …) — the chart window the funnel was found on. The engine scans several and reports only the strongest one per instrument.
Pre-order — a READY / DEVELOPING setup that hasn't triggered, so no order exists yet.
#Open / X posts — open IG positions in that name / how many X posts we've published for it.

Quality score

Quality (0–100) — combines tightness (up to 50: how compressed the funnel is vs AMP1), freshness (up to 30: how recently H3 formed) and symmetry (up to 20: how evenly the swings are spaced). 100 = very tight, fresh and symmetric.

Fundamentals (per company)

P/E (trailing / forward) — share price ÷ earnings per share (historic / forecast).
PEG — P/E relative to earnings growth (≈1 = fairly priced for its growth).
Price/Book · Price/Sales · EV/EBITDA — valuation multiples vs book value, revenue, and enterprise value/earnings.
EPS — earnings per share (trailing / forward).
Dividend yield / rate / payout ratio — annual dividend as a % of price / per share / as a % of earnings.
FCF · OCF — Free Cash Flow (cash left after capex) · Operating Cash Flow.
Gross / Operating / Profit margin — profit at each level as a % of revenue.
ROE / ROA — return on equity / on assets.
Revenue / Earnings growth — year-on-year growth rates.
Debt/Equity · Current ratio · Quick ratio — leverage and short-term liquidity.
Beta — volatility relative to the market (1 = moves with it, >1 = more).
Market cap · Revenue · EBITDA · 52-week range — size and trading range.

Broker & data

Broker analysis — analyst Buy/Hold counts plus the net upgrades − downgrades over the last 6 and 12 months.
Source (YF / IG) — where a stored price came from. IG (the broker feed) is treated as the truth and overrides Yahoo on disagreement.
double_checked — a stored daily price that a second source confirmed (IG agreeing with Yahoo).
Golden dataset — the audited, corrected price history kept in the database and verified daily.
