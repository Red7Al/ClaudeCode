32. Time: 22:40:00
    Request: P-01 BUG The winners endpoints are slow enough to dominate the Best Settings wait now the render freeze is fixed.
    Result: In progress -- the repeat-load cost is fixed and measured; the cold build remains. Cause found: both trigger-feature caches were single-slot carrying a "years" tag, so a hit required the tag to match and a call for a different window EVICTED the previous one instead of sitting beside it. Best Settings requests the annual window and then the deferred three-year window -- the two calls the page always makes together -- so neither was ever served warm, and _volscore_trigger_feature_map had no cache at all despite being called on every request alongside its sibling. That also explains the odd original reading, the longer window measuring FASTER: the shared _sqa_all_rows population happened to be warm from the call before it. Both caches are now keyed per window (bounded at four entries, since callers clamp years to 1-4) and the feature map has its own. Values and TTL are unchanged and each window still builds its own features, which is what keeps the three-year review from borrowing the annual map and showing blank VWAP/ATR cells; all callers are read-only. Measured live after deployment, in the order the page issues them: annual 32.8 s cold then 0.83 s warm, three-year 34.0 s cold then 1.28 s warm -- previously the second annual call would have been cold again because the three-year call had evicted it. Repeat loads are therefore about 25-40x faster. Remaining: the cold first build is still around 33 s and is unaffected by caching; serving a precomputed result on a schedule, as the Best Settings audit already does, is the next step. Note the measurements were taken while snapshot rebuild 32598244962 was scanning, so the cold figures may be pessimistic.
