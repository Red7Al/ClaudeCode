User Guide

Making your way around the web application

A tour of the tabs and how to find what you need

This guide walks you through the layout of the web application so you can find any feature quickly. Everything is reached from the row of tabs across the top of the page.

01

The top bar

The header carries the sign-in button (or your name once you are logged in), the light/dark theme toggle, and a refresh control. The tabs sit directly beneath it and stay pinned to the top as you scroll, so the navigation is always in reach.

What you see depends on who you are: a logged-out visitor sees a public subset; a signed-in user sees more; Support and Admin users see the operational tabs on top of that. If a tab you expect is missing, it is almost always because it is hidden for your role or has been switched off in Tab Visibility.

03

The signed-in tabs

Logging in unlocks the trading detail and your own account area:

  • Scanner Report and Instruments — now with the full set of columns (Direction, Quality, R:R, RVOL, VWAP, ATR, Volume Score).
  • Squeeze History — the full log of every setup the method has flagged and what happened next.
  • My Pre-orders and Pre-orders to my IG — the orders you have staged and the route that sends them to your IG account.
  • IG Account and Fees — your live broker position and the running cost breakdown across recent months.
  • My Activity — a record of the actions taken on your own account.
  • Configuration — your personal preferences and (for shared settings) the platform configuration.
04

The Operations and Settings groups

Two tabs act as parents for a sub-menu rather than a page of their own:

  • Settings groups Configuration and Markets (with their admin counterparts for those who have the role).
  • Operations groups the staff tools — Batch Activity, System Logs, Scheduled Jobs, User Management, Version History, X Posts and Change Requests.

Click the parent to reveal its children; a parent only appears when you can reach at least one tab inside it.

05

Documentation

The Documentation tab (where you found this guide) lists every guide as a downloadable Word document. The User Guides are available to everyone who is signed in; the Support and Operations material is reserved for Support and Admin roles.

If a tab is missing, open Configuration and check Tab Visibility — an admin can switch individual tabs on or off. A few tabs (such as Multi-Factor Momentum) are hidden by default until you opt in.
