Support — Adding Markets

Extending the scan universe with new markets

The set of markets the platform scans is managed from the Markets (Admin) tab. A market groups the instruments the scanner sweeps for The Squeeze.

01

Adding a market

  1. Open Settings, then Markets (Admin).
  2. Add the new market with its identifying details.
  3. Confirm which instruments belong to it, and whether it is tradeable or scan-only (see below).
  4. Save. The scanner picks it up on the next run; the Scanner "refresh a choice of markets" picker is driven from the canonical market list, so the new market appears there too.
02

Tradeable vs scan-only markets

Some markets are watched for the pattern but cannot be traded through IG because there are no matching IG epics. These are scan-only. For example, the planned Indian (BSE) and Chinese (Shenzhen) equities are scan-only — they surface setups but stage no orders.

A scan-only market must not be given IG epics it does not have. If setups from a market should never produce a stageable order, keep it scan-only.

The market universe itself is defined in config.py; the admin tab is the day-to-day surface for reviewing and adjusting it.
