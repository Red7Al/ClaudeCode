Operations — Version History

The record of what shipped

The Version History tab is the admin-facing log of what has changed on the platform over time. It is available to Admin.

01

What it shows

  • Dated entries summarising each change or release.
  • A category for each entry so related changes can be grouped (for example fixes, features, operations).
02

How it relates to Change Control

Change Control (the change-request files) is the detailed working record — every request, its priority and its completion note. Version History is the distilled summary: the headline of what shipped, without the full working detail. Read Change Requests to understand why something was done; read Version History to see what landed and when.

Keep Version History readable at a glance. The exhaustive detail belongs in the change-request completion notes; Version History is the summary a reviewer scans.
