What is the squeeze?

The pattern the whole platform is built around

The Squeeze is the single, repeatable price pattern the platform hunts for across hundreds of markets every day. This guide explains the idea in plain language. For the full method and its exact thresholds, see the plain-English system guide.

01

The coiling spring

After a market has had a strong run in one direction it often pauses and coils: the price swings get smaller and smaller, tightening into a narrower and narrower range, like a spring being wound up. When the spring finally releases, price tends to move quickly in the direction of the original trend.

Three things define a genuine squeeze: the highs keep getting lower, the lows keep getting higher, and the gap between them shrinks. The tighter that gap becomes, the more meaningful the eventual breakout.

02

It works both ways

After a strong rise, a coil that breaks upward is a bullish setup. After a strong fall, the same coil breaking downward is a bearish one. The method only ever calls the trade in the direction the market is already trending — it will not flag a bullish idea on a market that has been falling.

03

The five checks

A wobble is not a squeeze. A setup must clear five checks before it counts as real:

CheckWhat must be true
1. Prior trendA confirmed weekly trend in the trade's direction. Choppy, directionless markets are rejected.
2. Lower highsThree peaks, each lower than the last — the ceiling coming down.
3. Higher lowsThree dips, each higher than the last — the floor coming up.
4. Real squeezeThe range must tighten to under 35% of its starting width — at least 65% tighter than where it started.
5. Fresh breakoutThe breakout pivot must have formed recently (within the last 60 bars), not weeks ago.
04

From pattern to trade

Once the five checks pass, the three trade levels come straight from the shape: the entry is the breakout level itself; the safety exit (stop) sits just beyond the opposite side of the coil; and the target is one full amplitude — the height of the squeeze — projected from its middle in the breakout direction. Reward-to-risk is simply the distance to target divided by the distance to stop.

Two more gates then decide what happens: a setup must offer at least 3:1 reward-to-risk to be treated as tradeable, and must score at least 25/100 on pattern quality to be published publicly. Weaker setups stay on the internal watch-list.

The platform finds and ranks setups — it does not place trades by itself. Buying and selling only happens when you choose to stage an order to your own broker account.
