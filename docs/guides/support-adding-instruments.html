Support — Adding Instruments

Adding a single instrument to the universe

Instruments are the individual shares, indices and commodities the scanner watches. This guide covers adding one and where it then shows up.

01

Before you add

  • Check the instrument is not already tracked — the Instruments tab has a name/ticker search box for exactly this.
  • Decide which market it belongs to (see "Adding Markets").
  • For a tradeable instrument, have its IG epic to hand; a scan-only instrument needs none.
02

Adding the instrument

  1. Add the instrument under its market, with its ticker, name and sector.
  2. Supply the IG epic if it is tradeable; leave it unset for scan-only.
  3. Save, then confirm it appears in the Instruments tab with its stats populating on the next scan.
03

What populates automatically

Once the instrument is in the universe, the platform computes its stats on the next data refresh: the 52-week low/high, RVOL, VWAP, ATR, Volume Score, quality and reward-to-risk. You do not enter these by hand — they come from the price history.

If an instrument's stats stay blank, the usual cause is missing price history for its ticker. Confirm the ticker matches the data source before assuming a configuration fault.
